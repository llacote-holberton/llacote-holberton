# Hi there, I'm Laurent Lacôte 👋 🇫🇷 🇬🇧

**Technical Product Owner & IT Project Manager** based in Toulouse, France.

### Who am I
A lad who has always been passionate about sharing knowledge and know-hows and thus always gravitating towards Information Technologies, both in personal life (free software user, starting with Linux, from 2000) and professional one (>10 years working for free-software editor or consulting specialists).

### What path do I follow?
While I initially studied Law & Negociations, my path has always been rooted in Information Technologies, driven by both personal values and pragmatic benefit. Which is why I naturally favored a French Free Sofware Editor to start my professional carreer and evolved in that ecosystem for most of my career. I always aimed at helping free software development as I think it really is the best paradigm from a societal point of view in a majority of situations. And enjoyed as such promoting it directly (legal & marketing counselling for migration from proprietary to free, communication and training related to free-software products) and indirectly (deploying free software stacks, training end-users on free software based apps/websites, favoring free software whenever it makes sense).
What I felt missing was a rock-solid knowledge and know-how in actual development, as I rarely got the chance to directly contribute core myself. The main exception was my work at French Ministry of Finances, which confirmed my appetite for "getting my hands dirty".
Hence why I seized the chance of a time-limited mission ending to engage in a accelerated development training to expand and consolidate everything I had acquired from occasional hands-on in the field or personal experimentations.
My goal is to become skilled enough to develop business-focused applications in full autonomy to realize some pet projects I have in mind since eons. And be "harder better faster stronger" in the analysis and directions I provide when acting as a Project Manager or Architect.

### What can I do for you?
My multi-faceted profile mixing a solid Law background, a continuous technological survey and a permanent craving for expanding knowledge allowed me to handle several roles, either combined or sequentially, during my professional career: from defining business needs and planning during project boostrap to providing daily support to end-users, my combined experience enables me to provide a hawkeye's view on any project: both seeing the grand picture (business goals, main externalities, roadmap's milestones) and the fine grains of sand which may jam the whole project or snowball into huge crisis if not taken care of ASAP.

### What am I looking for?
As such, I am drawn to roles where the lines between requirement analysis, architecture input and hands-on project leadership are deliberately blurred — with more emphasis on shipping and team dynamics than on budget cycles. Ideally on open source or public-interest projects where the 'why' matters as much as the 'how'.
